# Meridian Outdoor Co.

An outdoor-gear maker in Denver: tents, shells, packs and camp kitchen. About 120 people;
the 18 in the prototype are the ones who use Birdy day to day. Meridian is on the Birdy
Business plan (`meridianoutdoor.com`).

## Projects in flight

- **Spring '27 Catalog** (Marketing): print catalog and site launch in January. Copy for
  tents and shelters is late; the landing page waits on a product-grid component nobody has
  picked up yet.
- **Checkout Redesign** (Product Design): cut checkout abandonment from 68% to 55%. Round
  2 research is in; address form error states are the critical path; the Safari
  autocomplete fix is blocked on an API key.
- **Supplier Portal** (Operations): self-serve inventory updates for about 40 vendors,
  piloting with three in February.

## How they use Birdy

Tasks live in Birdy; files live in project folders. Project folders are private to the
teams working on them, while Company and Marketing folders are open to everyone.
Photography is shared with a guest from Northlight Studio.

Source of truth for the data: `Code/src/mocks/seed/`.
